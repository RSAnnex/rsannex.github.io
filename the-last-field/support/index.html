Support

If something interrupts your journey, we will help you return to the field.

Contact us

Email support@rsannex.com. Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, the level name and a short description of what happened. A screenshot is useful when possible.

Restore Remove Adverts

Open Options on the title screen and choose Restore Purchase. Make sure the device is signed in with the Apple ID originally used for the purchase.

Advertising privacy choices

Open Options and choose Privacy Choices to review the advertising consent options available in your region.

Music and sound

Music can be switched on or off from Options. Choose Music lets you select a particular piece; otherwise the game plays the collection in a shuffled order.

Progress

The River Bond becomes available after all twenty field stages are conquered. If progress appears to be missing, do not delete the app before contacting support because local progress may be removed with it.
