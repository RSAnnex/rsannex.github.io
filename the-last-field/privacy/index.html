Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

RS Annex Ltd respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use The Last Field on iPhone or iPad.

Information we collect

RS Annex Ltd does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, postal address or telephone number to play the game. Game progress, preferences and purchase status may be stored locally on your device.

Advertising

The free version of the game uses Google AdMob to provide adverts, including optional rewarded adverts. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from an IP address, advertising interactions, diagnostics and other usage information to provide, measure, secure and—where you have consented—personalise advertising.

Where required, the game asks for your advertising privacy choices. You can review those choices from the Options screen. You can also purchase Remove Adverts; if enabled, ordinary adverts are disabled.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and in Google’s explanation of how it uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. RS Annex Ltd does not receive your full payment-card details. Apple may provide the game with transaction information needed to confirm and restore a purchase. Apple’s handling of information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and consent behaviour may vary according to age, region and the privacy choices available on the device.

Data retention and control

Local game data remains on your device until it is cleared or the app is removed. Information processed by Apple, Google or their partners is retained according to their respective policies. Available advertising privacy choices can be reviewed inside the game.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, applicable law or the services it uses change. The revised date will appear at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@rsannex.com.
