The system is under attack

Two processors. One circuit board.
No room for bugs.

Pulse Guard turns the inside of a computer into a frantic arcade battlefield. Tiny invaders follow individual circuit paths towards the processors while you rotate the guns around each chip and fire straight down the traces.

01

Guard both sides

The CPU and GPU are attacked independently. Watch both halves of the board and decide where the danger is building.

02

Line up the traces

Rotate the processor defences, point each gun down a connection and intercept bugs before they reach the pins.

03

Unleash the pulse

Send a powerful pulse across the data bus to disrupt the opposite side and buy yourself a few vital seconds.

It starts controlled. It doesn't stay that way.

New paths, faster bugs and increasingly frenzied attacks turn a simple defence into a battle to keep the whole system alive.
