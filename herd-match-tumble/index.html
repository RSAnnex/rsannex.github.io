Match the herd. Beat the tumble.

Herd Match Tumble combines satisfying match-three puzzles with a falling-pair challenge starring a colourful herd of cattle.

Two ways to play

A simple idea with plenty of challenge.

Choose a relaxed matching game or keep your cool as new cattle tumble into the field.

1

Original mode

Swap neighbouring cattle to create matches, trigger cascades and chase the highest score you can.

2

Tumbler mode

Steer and rotate falling pairs into place. Keep matching before the herd reaches the top.

3

Meet the herd

Play with six recognisable cattle breeds, each recreated as a cheerful, distinctive game tile.
