Controls, common questions and a direct route to help.

How to play

Original mode

Tap one cattle tile and then a neighbouring tile to swap them. Create a row or column of three or more matching cattle to clear them and score points.

Tumbler mode

Use the left and right buttons to move the falling pair. Use the rotate button to change its orientation and the down button to drop it faster. Match cattle before the stack reaches the top.

How do I pause?

Tap the pause button at the bottom-right of the game screen. You can resume or return to the mode selection screen.

Troubleshooting

The game has stopped responding

Close Herd Match Tumble from the iPhone app switcher and open it again. If the issue continues, restart the iPhone and make sure the latest version of the game is installed.

I found a bug

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, the game mode you were playing and what happened immediately before the problem. A screenshot is also useful.

Will deleting the app remove my progress?

Deleting an app can remove locally stored settings and scores. Avoid deleting it as a troubleshooting step unless you are comfortable losing local progress.
