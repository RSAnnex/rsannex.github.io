Help with Cowdoku for iPhone and iPad.

Need help?

If Cowdoku is not behaving as expected, close and reopen the app first. If the problem continues, contact RS Annex with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, level number and a short description.

Common questions

How do I play?

Place one cow in every row, column and coloured paddock. Cows cannot touch horizontally, vertically or diagonally. Tap an empty square once to add an X and twice to place a cow.

Why did I lose a star?

Each level begins with three stars. An incorrect cow placement is caught immediately and removes one star. The level ends if all three are lost.

How does the daily challenge work?

Each date creates four puzzles: 6×6, 7×7, 8×8 and 9×9. Complete all four to earn the gold star on your daily calendar. Earlier dates remain available.

My progress disappeared.

Stars, settings, daily results and best times are stored locally and can be lost if the app or its data is removed.

Does the game show adverts?

Cowdoku uses Google AdMob and may show an interstitial advert after every third finished game. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I report a bug?

Email rsannexltd@proton.me. A screenshot or short recording is especially useful.
