Privacy Policy

Cowdoku · RS Annex Ltd · Last updated 12 September 2026

1. Overview

RS Annex Ltd respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use Cowdoku on iPhone or iPad.

Cowdoku does not require an RS Annex account and RS Annex does not ask for your name, postal address or telephone number to play.

2. Advertising

Cowdoku displays interstitial advertising supplied by Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads, normally after every third finished game. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device or app identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions and diagnostic information to provide adverts, measure performance and prevent fraud.

The data handled by Google can depend on your device settings, region, consent choices and the advert served. Read Google's Privacy Policy and its advertising technologies information.

3. Permission and consent choices

Cowdoku remains playable if advertising personalisation or tracking permission is unavailable or declined. If a system permission or legally required advertising consent choice is presented, you may accept or decline it and review relevant permissions later in iOS or iPadOS Settings.

4. Local game data

Cowdoku stores settings, level progress, stars, best times, daily-challenge results and the advert frequency counter locally on your device. RS Annex does not receive this local gameplay data.

5. Diagnostics and platform services

Apple, Google advertising services or system services integrated into the app may process technical and diagnostic information under their own terms and policies. This may include device characteristics, crash information and performance data.

6. Children

Cowdoku is a general-audience puzzle game. RS Annex does not knowingly ask children to submit personal information directly through the game. Advertising behaviour may vary by territory, device settings and platform requirements.

7. app-ads.txt

RS Annex publishes a file identifying authorised digital advertising sellers at rsannex.com/app-ads.txt. It contains no player personal information.

8. External links

The game or website may link to services such as Apple or Google. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

9. Contact

For privacy questions about Cowdoku or information held directly by RS Annex, email rsannexltd@proton.me. Because Cowdoku has no RS Annex account, RS Annex may hold little or no information capable of identifying a player.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when the game, advertising configuration or legal requirements change. The latest version will remain available here.

Advertising transparency: Cowdoku's App Store developer website should use the RS Annex domain so advertising systems can locate https://rsannex.com/app-ads.txt. The file belongs at the domain root, not inside the /cowdoku/ folder.
