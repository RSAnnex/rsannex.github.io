IMPORTANT — ADMOB / APP-ADS.TXT
-------------------------------
Do NOT place a separate app-ads.txt inside /fill-the-barn/.
Google's AdMob crawler looks at the hostname from the App Store developer/marketing website and checks the root domain.

Your existing authorised seller file should remain here:
https://rsannex.com/app-ads.txt

For the App Store listing, use an RS Annex website URL on rsannex.com (for example the Pack the Barn page as the Marketing URL). The crawler then derives rsannex.com and checks the root app-ads.txt.

- Apple App Tracking Transparency if tracking permission is requested
